# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project management approach that emphasizes clear ownership, small deliverable increments, measurable outcomes, and regular stakeholder communication. Work moves from initiation and planning into execution, risk tracking, release readiness, and retrospectives so teams can continuously improve how they deliver customer value.

## Process Summary
- **Initiation:** clarify the business need, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, and go/no-go decision.
- **Planning:** break approved work into prioritized backlog items, define milestones, document dependencies, and align responsibilities.
- **Execution & tracking:** manage delivery through regular team rhythms, small pull requests, testing, and milestone tracking.
- **Risk management & communication:** maintain a risk register, share regular status updates, and use clear escalation paths when blockers arise.
- **Release & deployment:** verify acceptance criteria, confirm release readiness, deploy safely, and communicate outcomes.
- **Retrospectives & improvement:** capture lessons learned after sprints, releases, and incidents, then turn them into actionable follow-up work.
- **Roles & personas:** define how project managers, product managers, and developers contribute to planning, delivery, and communication.
